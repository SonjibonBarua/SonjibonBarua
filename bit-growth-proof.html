BIT / VERIFIED GROWTH PROOF

From reach to real enquiries.

This page converts the Meta Business Suite screenshots supplied for the portfolio into a transparent performance case study. The figures below are platform-reported values for the periods shown in the screenshots; they are not estimates.

Evidence basis: Meta Business Suite screenshots provided by the portfolio owner. Main annual snapshot: Jan 1–Oct 9, 2026. Momentum snapshot: Sep 11–Oct 8, 2026. No completed-customer conversion is claimed because Meta shows 0 contacts with orders in the annual snapshot.

02 / ORGANIC + PAID

Paid created scale. Organic still delivered 2.0M views.

Meta attributes 6.08M views to ads and 1.996M to organic activity in the Jan 1–Oct 9 snapshot. That is approximately 75.3% paid and 24.7% organic by views.

Views by source

Platform-reported view attribution.

Contact acquisition mix

New contacts in the same annual snapshot.

Marketing interpretation: the paid layer is doing the heavy lifting for scalable reach and contact acquisition, while organic activity contributes a meaningful 2.0M views. The strongest next optimization question is therefore not simply “more reach?” but “which paid creatives and audiences are producing the highest-quality admissions conversations?”
